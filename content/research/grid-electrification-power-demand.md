## Beyond the data center headline

Data center load growth is real, but it is also the most-discussed and best-modeled part of the demand story. Industrial electrification and vehicle charging load are growing from a smaller base but with less cyclicality, and current utility integrated resource plans are inconsistent in how they capture this.

## The grid is the constraint, not generation

Interconnection queues, not generation capacity, are the practical bottleneck across most of the regions we cover. Transmission-focused capital deployment appears underappreciated relative to the attention generation technology choices receive.
