## Demand is outrunning permits

Build-to-suit industrial demand near reshored manufacturing corridors is running well ahead of entitlement timelines, particularly for power-dense sites near existing substation capacity. That bottleneck, not construction cost, is the binding constraint in the markets we track most closely.

## Where the scarcity value concentrates

Sites with existing heavy power infrastructure are commanding a premium that has grown faster than headline industrial rent indices suggest, because those indices weight a broader and less power-constrained set of properties.
